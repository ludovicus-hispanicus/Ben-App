# BEn News

## 2026-10-29 — Coming in the next release
The next BEn release brings:

- **Kraken 7.1** with a new CUSAS model (6.2% character error rate on held-out CUSAS lines, down from 7.9%).
- **Layout detection inside CuReD:** find the regions of a dictionary page (entries, headers, page numbers) and send one to OCR with a click. Layout training moves into a CuReD tab and offers YOLO26.
- **Ollama models found automatically:** CuReD lists the installed models that can read images, Ollama's cloud models, and models to download, with their size.
- **Current model lists from Gemini, Claude, OpenAI and Grok**, including "always latest" options.
- **Easier navigation:** a back button with the path of where you are, and the browser's back button follows it.
